ABOUT THE GAMES

Sport Brings Us Together

The Commonwealth Games is an international multi-sport event bringing together athletes from Commonwealth nations and territories.

Glasgow 2026 created a more compact Games model, using existing sporting infrastructure and concentrating competition across Glasgow.
